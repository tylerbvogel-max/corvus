# Angel / Operator Target Map

Last updated: 2026-04-13

## Strategy

You are a solo builder with no prior fundraising track record. Going directly to tier-1 VCs is low-probability and high-signal-burn (a "no" from Sequoia today is a harder "yes" from Sequoia in 18 months). The higher-EV path is:

1. **Operator angels first** — people who have shipped the exact problem, write $25K–$100K checks on conviction, and open doors later.
2. **Credibility co-founder** — this single hire changes the fundraising arithmetic more than any deck revision.
3. **Institutional seed** only after you have 2–3 paying design partners and the co-founder.

This doc maps the three networks that matter and how to reach them. It does *not* list specific individuals — those change and become out-of-date fast; the categories and the outreach approach are what's durable.

---

## Network 1: Palantir Mafia

**Why this network**: Palantir alumni are the single most active founder-and-angel ecosystem for ontology/provenance/FDE-pattern companies. The "pay-it-forward" norm is explicit — alumni actively angel into new alumni ventures (see Conduct seed round, Anduril's early cap table). Forward Deployed Venture Capital (Mark Scianna) closed a $45M fund in 2025 specifically for defense/security.

**Target archetypes** (search for these titles on LinkedIn, filter by "ex-Palantir"):
- Former Forward Deployed Engineer, now founder or VP Eng at a Series A/B startup
- Former Palantir Ontology PM
- Former Palantir Federal / defense-sector FDE (aligns with `corvus-aero` tenant)
- Nabeel Qureshi–style operator-writers who angel actively

**Outreach pattern**:
- Cold-email with a **3-minute video demo** of Corvus doing the ontology + provenance thing Foundry does, in a multi-tenant deploy they can't get from Palantir. Technical specificity reads as credibility.
- Subject line format that works: "ex-Palantir FDE — would love your take on a Foundry-for-mid-market thing I built"
- Ask for feedback first, check second. The ask becomes natural after they engage.

**Warm intro paths**:
- Forward Deployed Venture Capital partners (they map the network professionally)
- Anduril early employees (many are angels now)
- The `corvus-aero` tenant is defense-aerospace — if you can get a defense-adjacent Palantir alum as first check, the narrative writes itself

## Network 2: Databricks / Snowflake Ventures + alumni

**Why this network**: Databricks Ventures and Snowflake Ventures are actively investing in governance-era data infrastructure (Theom $20M Series A, Uniphore $260M Series F, Voyage AI). Their investment thesis is explicit: intelligent + governed AI is the next layer. They do strategic checks ($500K–$5M) and provide distribution credibility.

**Target archetypes**:
- Databricks Ventures / Snowflake Ventures partners (direct, public email)
- Ex-Databricks Unity Catalog PMs
- Ex-Snowflake Horizon / governance product leads
- Former field CTOs from both companies (they see every buyer's governance pain)

**Outreach pattern**:
- These are strategic investors — they need to see fit with the partner ecosystem. Frame Corvus as **governed context layer that sits above both Databricks and Snowflake**, not competitive with either.
- Ventures arms typically co-invest, not lead. Line them up *after* an angel lead has been committed.

**Warm intro paths**:
- Theom, Voyage AI founders (they recently walked this path; the playbook is fresh)
- Databricks Ventures has a public portfolio page; 2nd-degree LinkedIn connections to portfolio founders are the intro route

## Network 3: Glean + Writer + governance-adjacent founders

**Why this network**: Founders of adjacent companies understand the wedge fastest. They won't see Corvus as competition (different ICP, different grain of provenance), and they have investor networks you want.

**Target archetypes**:
- Early Glean employees (not Arvind — too senior now), especially founding engineers who left to angel
- Writer early team
- Founders of Atlan, Collibra, Alation alumni companies
- Knowledge-graph-specific founders: ex-Neo4j, ex-Stardog, ex-TigerGraph enterprise sellers

**Outreach pattern**:
- Lead with the wedge differentiation, not the overlap. "I'm building the governance layer Glean doesn't have — want your take on whether the positioning is honest."
- Peers respond to peer framing, not pitch framing.

## Network 4: Thematic AI-governance angels

Independent of the three networks above, there is a growing cohort of people angel-investing specifically on the "AI governance will matter" thesis:

- AI safety / interpretability researchers who have exited (OpenAI / Anthropic / DeepMind alumni)
- Former CISOs of regulated-industry SaaS companies
- Legal-tech founders who have shipped provenance products (e.g., Hebbia, Harvey alumni)

These are the highest-conviction, smallest-check investors. They move fast and can close in a single meeting.

## The credibility co-founder search

**Highest-ROI single action.** Target profile:

- 5–10 years at one of: Palantir (Ontology/FDE), Databricks (Unity Catalog), Collibra, Atlan, Immuta, Alation, Stardog, Neo4j
- Has shipped a governance or lineage product in production
- Either (a) currently at a big-company post, looking for founder opportunity, or (b) 2–3 years into a startup that isn't working and is open to pivoting in
- Complementary to you: if you are the systems/graph architect, they are the GTM-credibility face

**Where to find**:
- Run Meeter: post about building Corvus on LinkedIn weekly; the right co-founder will DM you within 3 months if your posts are technically dense
- On Deck / Antler / South Park Commons co-founder matching
- Angel meetings double as co-founder auditions — several people you pitch will want in as operators, not just checks

## 90-day action plan

| Weeks | Action | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Record the 3-minute governed-context demo (policy change mid-session → audit trail). Post to LinkedIn with technical write-up. | Passive inbound from right-profile operators |
| 3–4 | Reach out to 20 Palantir alumni via LinkedIn, lead with demo + feedback ask | 3–5 conversations, 1–2 warm intros |
| 5–6 | Convert 2 existing tenants (`corvus-aero` defense design partner, `corvus-hedge` fintech) into paid pilots — even $5K/mo counts for the narrative | First revenue, first case study |
| 7–8 | Co-founder conversations running in parallel; angel conversations warming | Shortlist of 2–3 potential co-founders |
| 9–12 | First angel check closed ($25K–$100K from operator), snowball the rest off that reference | $500K–$1M soft-circled |
| 13+ | Close angel round, hire co-founder or commit to solo + strong advisor bench, begin building toward institutional seed pitch | Ready to approach Glean-class investors in 6 more months |

## What *not* to do

- **Don't cold-email Sequoia / KP / Lightspeed now.** You get one shot per partner; save it for when you have design partners and a co-founder.
- **Don't raise on SAFE from strangers.** Every early investor should either be strategic (operator) or have an explicit door they'll open.
- **Don't over-index on the biomimetic framing.** Lead with the outcome (governed context), explain the mechanism (neuromorphic graph) only when asked.
- **Don't accept a lead from a fund that won't follow-on at seed.** The angel round's job is to graduate you to seed; angels with no institutional relationships are dead-end capital.
