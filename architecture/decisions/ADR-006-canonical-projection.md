# ADR-006: Project skills and standing capsules from canonical graph state

- Status: Accepted retrospectively
- Recorded: 2026-07-29

## Context

Harnesses benefit from cheap pull-based playbooks and startup policy. Hand-made
files lack graph provenance and can survive after their source knowledge changes.

## Decision

Lessons remain canonical in PostgreSQL. Compiler and projection services
generate skills, manifests, charter, and self-model capsules with source-neuron
lineage.

## Consequences

- Generated capabilities can be retracted and rebuilt.
- Projection freshness and path isolation require verification.
- Large lesson bodies graduate to pull-on-demand delivery rather than ambient
  prompt injection.

## Alternatives considered

- Hand-author harness skills independently of memory.
- Inject every lesson into every prompt.
